Prescription Verification Policy

Khalsa Medicos Patient Safety Protocol

As a licensed retail chemist and druggist, Khalsa Medicos adheres strictly to national Drug Regulations (Schedule H and Schedule H1 drugs):

  • All prescription medications require a clear, valid prescription issued by a registered medical practitioner.
  • Prescriptions must specify the patient name, doctor registration number, diagnosis/dose, and date.
  • For online reservations, you may present your prescription slip at the counter or share a copy via our WhatsApp helpline (+91 98881-02937).
  • No prescription medicines will be dispensed without on-duty pharmacist verification.
